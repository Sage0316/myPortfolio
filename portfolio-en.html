01 · About

Human evidence should
shape how technology behaves.

During my master's training in psychology, I studied depression, food addiction, and problematic internet and smartphone use among adolescents. My work included psychometric validation and the design and evaluation of behavioral intervention programs.

I then spent six years delivering EAP, HRD, and organizational culture initiatives for corporate and public-sector clients, developing the ability to structure human behavior and experience and translate insight into interventions that work in practice.

I now bring that foundation into HAI/HRI. I turn observable signals and interaction context into behavioral rules for AI, then build and test the resulting systems. EIRA and my robotics work connect human-centered research, interaction design, implementation, and field validation in one end-to-end practice.

01 · Flagship personal project

EIRA
Proactive Physical AI Companion

A camera-based conversational agent that uses facial expression, gaze, upper-body movement, and dialogue context to initiate interaction at an appropriate moment—without waiting for the user to type first.

HAI / HRIGPT LiveVisionSolo project
Core interaction principle
Reach out first.
Respect silence and rejection.

Interaction Design

  • Silence mode after 10 seconds without a response
  • Immediate waiting state after explicit rejection
  • Resume only when gaze and direct address by name co-occur
  • Optional speech stop when another person appears

System

  • Low / steady / positive state signals
  • Multi-angle face enrollment and new-face checks
  • Single-call vision backend architecture
  • Real-time dialogue powered by GPT Live

Validation

  • Built independently over approximately one month
  • Field-tested in cafés, stores, and bookstores
  • Refined long gaps, rapid speech, and false positives
  • Moved policy and memory logic server-side
